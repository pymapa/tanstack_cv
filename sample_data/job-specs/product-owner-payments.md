# Request for proposal: Product Owner, payments platform renewal

**Client:** Northwind Finance Oy (fictional)
**Location:** Helsinki, hybrid (two days a week on site)
**Start:** 2026-11-02
**Duration:** 9 months, with an option to extend by 6 months
**Allocation:** 100%
**Proposals due:** 2026-10-09

## Background

Northwind Finance is replacing its card and account payment platform. The work runs in
three agile teams (about 20 people) that build new services on Azure while the old system
is still in use. We need an experienced Product Owner to own the backlog of the customer-facing
payments team and to work closely with the business, architects and the other teams.

## Responsibilities

- Own and prioritize the product backlog, and write clear user stories with acceptance criteria.
- Run backlog refinement, sprint planning and reviews together with the Scrum Master.
- Translate regulatory and business needs (PSD2, strong customer authentication) into
  requirements that the team can deliver.
- Coordinate dependencies with two other teams in a SAFe-style release train.
- Report progress and risks to the steering group.

## Must have

- At least 5 years as a Product Owner or in a similar product role in agile teams.
- Experience in the finance or banking industry.
- Hands-on experience with Scrum and backlog tools such as Jira.
- Fluent Finnish and English.

## Nice to have

- Experience with payment systems, card payments or open banking APIs.
- Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO) or SAFe POPM.
- Experience in large system renewals where old and new systems run in parallel.
- Understanding of cloud services, preferably Microsoft Azure.

## Proposal

Send one or two CVs per role, a short description of how the person fits the need, and an
hourly rate. CVs must be in English.
